# Launch

**Live (launch URL):** https://theguysaccount.github.io/audiosage-podcaster-dashboards/. Nothing here needs DNS to work.

## Domain

`audiosage.org` (Jack's portfolio match) already serves an AudioSage site from SiteGround (checked 2026-11-22). It is
not replaced. Proposed: `dashboards.audiosage.org` (one CNAME). Alternatives if Jack prefers a separate name:
`podcaststats.org`-style new registrations (unchecked; needs his OK to buy). Steps: OPERATIONS.md, key 1.

## Distribution (machine channels only; nothing is posted as a person)

- **Search pages.** "<show> podcast stats: signature phrases, talk pace, topics" for each show and
  "<show> vs <show>: podcast stats compared" for all 66 pairs, with share cards, structured data, canonical URLs and a
  sitemap. IndexNow on every deploy; the account root robots.txt lists the sitemap.
- **Share loop.** Every dashboard and comparison has copy and share buttons and its own share image ("Who talks
  faster?"), the thing fans argue about.
- **Open data.** `data/stats.json` (CC BY 4.0) and an open-source method, for journalists and researchers.
- **GitHub discovery.** Repo topics: podcast, podcasts, podcast-analytics, transcripts, nlp, data-visualization,
  github-pages.

## First 1,000 users

No outreach in machine mode. Visitors arrive from "<show> stats / signature phrase / vs" searches and shared comparison
links. Watch `compare_pick` and shares once analytics is on (OPERATIONS.md, key 3).
