# Product: AudioSage

**The user.** Podcast fans who want to compare their favorite hosts, and journalists or researchers who want quick,
checkable facts about what a show talks about. Later: advertisers choosing shows.

**The painful job.** Knowing what a show is really like (its verbal habits, pace, topics, guests) means listening for
hours. Podcast charts rank popularity, not content.

**The promise.** Playful, checkable stats on what top podcasters actually say, each phrase linked to the exact moment.

**The wedge.** Searchable pages for "<show> podcast stats" and "<show> vs <show>", and shareable comparison cards
(each pair has its own page and share image), which is what fans argue about.

**Why now.** Hosting platforms (iHeart's Omny, Transistor, Spreaker, RSS.com and others) now publish timed transcripts
in podcast feeds (`podcast:transcript`), so a dashboard can be built without recording or transcribing anything.

**The one activation metric.** A visitor compares two shows (`compare_pick`), then shares or copies the comparison.

**First revenue event.** A sponsor on the leaderboard (`SPONSOR_URL`); later, advertiser reports on other hosting.

**What v1 deliberately won't do.** No bias, belief or stance scores (defamation risk, and not checkable). No full
transcripts. No transcription of audio. No shows that don't publish their own transcripts. No political commentary
shows in the first 12 (they qualify; see DECISIONS.md).
